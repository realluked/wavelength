# Wavelength

Duolingo-style revision for **Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625)**, made by **realluked**.

**Play it here: https://realluked.github.io/wavelength/**

- 67 bite-size lessons covering every topic: motion, forces and energy; thermal physics; waves; electricity and magnetism; nuclear physics; space physics; and practical skills
- Core or Extended: switch any time; Extended (Supplement) content is clearly tagged
- Worked calculations: type your number, get it checked, and see the full working when you slip
- Diagrams drawn for every topic: graphs, circuits, ray diagrams, fields, meters and more
- Exam practice: structured questions with mark schemes and model answers, two full theory papers, an Alternative to Practical paper, and a 40-question multiple-choice paper
- AI examiner for written answers, marked point by point
- Recall deck with spaced repetition for every equation, definition and key fact, plus Equation Rush and Unit Rush
- Formula sheet, definitions, exam guide, exam countdown, a daily plan and an XP league
- Optional account: play as a guest, or log in to sync progress across devices (one account also works on Bitwise and Inkling)

All questions, explanations and diagrams are original. Not affiliated with Cambridge International.

Also by realluked: [Bitwise](https://realluked.github.io/bitwise/) for IGCSE Computer Science and [Inkling](https://realluked.github.io/inkling/) for IGCSE English.
